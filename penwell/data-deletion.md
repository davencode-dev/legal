# Penwell — Data Deletion Policy

**Last updated: 9 May 2026**

Penwell is a privacy-first application. The vast majority of your data (journal entries, habits, moods) is stored locally on your device in an encrypted database.

## How to delete your data

### 1. Local Data (Entries, Habits, Moods)
You can instantly and permanently delete all your journal entries and app data by opening the Penwell app, navigating to **Settings**, and tapping **"Delete All Data"**. This action is irreversible as the data only exists on your device.

### 2. Cloud/AI Data ("Deep Insight")
Any text sent to our AI features (when you explicitly use the "Deep Insight" feature) is processed ephemerally and automatically deleted by our AI providers (Anthropic) within 7 days.

### 3. Purchase History & Analytics (RevenueCat)
If you have made a premium purchase, an anonymous purchase history is associated with your anonymous device ID via RevenueCat. If you wish to request the deletion of this anonymous purchase history, please send an email to **vendrell.dev@gmail.com** with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 14 days.
