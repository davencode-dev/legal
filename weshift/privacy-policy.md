# WeShift — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 15 July 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **WeShift** (the "app") handles your shift
schedule and related information.

WeShift is developed and operated by an independent developer based in Spain.
Contact: **daventorn@gmail.com**.

## Summary in one paragraph

WeShift works fully offline with no account required. Your shifts, rest-rule
settings, appointments, holidays, and any notes you type are **stored locally on
your device** (AsyncStorage / SQLite) and never leave your phone. If you choose to
turn on the optional account sync/sharing feature, that same data is mirrored to
our backend (Supabase) over an encrypted connection so it can sync across your
devices and be shared, item by item, with people you explicitly invite.

## What the app stores on your device

- Shifts, appointments, holidays, and any titles/notes you enter for them.
- Rest-rule thresholds and recurrence patterns you configure.
- All of the above stays on-device and is never transmitted anywhere unless you
  enable sync.

## What's stored on our servers (only if you create an account)

If you enable sync/sharing, WeShift uses Supabase, Inc. as its backend provider
to store:

- **Email address**, to create and authenticate your account.
- **Calendar items you sync**: shifts, appointments, holidays, titles and notes.
- **Connections and groups**: who you've invited to share with, and the
  visibility level (full detail, busy-only, or hidden) set per shared item.
- **Conflict requests**: proposed times from a connection and their
  accept/decline status.

Nothing marked "hidden" is ever visible to anyone else. Data in transit is
encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

## What we don't do

- No ads, no third-party advertising SDKs.
- No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for their own
  marketing or advertising purposes.
- We do not collect health records or medical information — the shift and
  rest-rule data you enter is scheduling information you control, not clinical
  data.

## Data retention and deletion

You can disable sync at any time in Settings; previously synced data remains in
your account until deleted. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data
from your device. To request deletion of your account and any data stored on our
servers, email **daventorn@gmail.com** — we will delete it within 30 days.

## Children's privacy

WeShift is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from
children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected
by updating the "Last updated" date above.
